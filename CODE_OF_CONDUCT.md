# Community conduct

Treat participants respectfully, regardless of background or experience. Focus
feedback on ideas and code. Be constructive, accept corrections, and respect
others' privacy.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual content directed at others,
and publication of private information are not welcome. Do not use issues or
pull requests for unsolicited promotion.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict
participation. Report abuse through GitHub's report-abuse controls; do not repeat
private or harmful material in a public issue. These expectations apply to this
repository's issues, pull requests and other project discussions.
